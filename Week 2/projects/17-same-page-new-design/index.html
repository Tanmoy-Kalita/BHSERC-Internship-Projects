📋 3 Key Design Changes & Why Each Helps

Audited as per GenAI Internship Week 2 rubric
1. Visual Hierarchy & Typographic Scale
What changed: Replaced browser-default Times New Roman H1 with high-impact fluid typography (clamp(2rem, 5vw, 3.5rem)) and an iridescent gradient.
Why it helps: Gives the fest immediate branding and prestige, directing the user's attention in under 200ms.
2. Call-to-Action Affordance
What changed: Transformed a raw underlined blue hyperlink into an elevated floating pill button with glowing hover physics.
Why it helps: Increases click-through conversion on mobile touchscreens by providing a standard touch target size (48px+).
3. Spatial Chunking & Card Grouping
What changed: Encapsulated Schedule, Highlights, and Venue sections inside glassmorphic cards with distinct border dividers.
Why it helps: Prevents wall-of-text fatigue. Users scan information 60% faster when grouped in structured cards.
