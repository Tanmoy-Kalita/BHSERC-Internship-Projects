Prompts for One-Minute Reel Cut (Project 15)

=== Prompt 1: Initialize One-Minute Reel Cut ===
I want to build a video editing tool that takes a 5-minute raw video and produces a 60-second vertical reel. Must remove silences and filler words, crop to 9:16 aspect ratio keeping face in frame, burn in captions with highlighted words, add title card and audio fades, show edit plan before cutting. Rule: use only video user filmed themselves. Best on a paid agent plan.

=== Prompt 2: Implement Transcript with Timestamps ===
Generate a transcript of the video with exact timestamps for each sentence. Show the edit plan highlighting which sections to remove (silences, "umm"s, repeated sentences).

=== Prompt 3: Implement Silence and Filler Word Removal ===
Auto-detect and remove silent pauses and filler words ("umm", "ahh", "like", "you know"). The reel should flow naturally.

=== Prompt 4: Implement Crop to 9:16 Aspect Ratio ===
Crop the video to vertical 9:16 format (for phone/reels). Keep the user's face in frame using tracking or padding.

=== Prompt 5: Implement Captions with Highlighted Words ===
Add captions that match the speech. Highlight the current word being spoken as the video plays.

=== Prompt 6: Implement Title Card and Audio Fades ===
Add a title card at the beginning and audio fade-in/out at the end.

=== Prompt 7: Show Edit Plan Before Cutting ===
Display the planned cuts (which timestamps to remove) and let the user approve before the final video is rendered.

=== Prompt 8: Test 60 Seconds or Shorter ===
Verify the final reel is 60 seconds or shorter.

=== Prompt 9: Test Captions Match Speech ===
Hand-check 20 lines of captions against the actual speech to verify they match.

=== Prompt 10: Test No Sentence Cut Off ===
Verify no sentence is cut off mid-way in the final reel.

=== Prompt 11: Test on Phone ===
Verify the reel plays correctly on a phone.

=== Prompt 12: Test User-Filmed Only ===
Ensure only video the user filmed themselves is used (no stock footage or external videos).
