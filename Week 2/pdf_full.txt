Preview: an example only. Your design can differ, but it needs every feature below.
What to build
1
Step 1, without a skill. Build a simple page
for a college fest, café, coaching institute or
your own idea. Use no design skill. Save it
as before.html.
2
Step 2, add a design skill. Install
Impeccable. You can also browse skills.sh
for others, such as frontend-design or ui-ux-
pro-max.
3
Step 3, redesign. Use the skill on the same
page. Save it as after.html.
Done when
You have both before.html and after.html.
The words in both files match.
The redesign works on a phone.
You can name 3 design changes and why
each one helps.
Small hint
Ask the skill to critique your page first, then fix the biggest problems one at a time. For a bonus, try a second
design skill from skills.sh on the same page and compare.
